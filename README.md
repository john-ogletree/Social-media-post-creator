# OmniPost - Community Templates

**OmniPost** is a community-driven web tool that creates beautiful 9:16 quote graphics optimized for social media. With live preview, customizable themes, and one-click templates, it makes creating visually consistent posts quick and effortless.

## What is OmniPost?

OmniPost generates graphics in a **9:16 vertical aspect ratio** (1080x1920) with a centered **4:5 inner card block** that contains your quote text. This dual-ratio design gives you the best of both worlds:

- **9:16 Canvas:** Perfect for TikTok, Instagram Stories, Reels, YouTube Shorts, and vertical social media
- **4:5 Inner Block:** The card itself maintains a classic square-ish ratio that works beautifully for Instagram Feed posts, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Pinterest

The 4:5 inner card creates a focused, framed look that draws attention to your quote while the surrounding 9:16 canvas provides breathing room and a premium, branded feel. This design philosophy ensures your content looks native and polished whether viewed full-screen on mobile or in a feed.

The tool features a live preview canvas where you type your quote and see it rendered instantly.

## Why is this different from the rest?

OmniPost stands out because it is:

1. **Purpose-Built for Mobile-First Platforms:** Most design tools were built for desktop and adapted for mobile. OmniPost starts with 9:16, ensuring your content looks native and polished on every vertical platform.

2. **Perfectly Balanced Composition:** The 4:5 inner card sits centered on the 9:16 canvas, creating a clean, balanced composition that works across every platform. Your text stays readable and prominent.

3. **Frictionless and Fast:** No complex menus or templates to wrestle with. Type your message, see a live preview, and download your graphic. Built for speed, perfect for creators who need to post on the go.

4. **Community Templates Included:** Choose from six curated templates (Minimal, Vibrant, Dark, Ocean, Sunset, Cyberpunk) that auto-apply color schemes with one click.

5. **Automated Metadata:** Dynamic tags for day of year, progress percentage, date, and year sync automatically with your system clock.

6. **Lightweight and Accessible:** Web-based with no downloads or installations. Works directly in your browser from any device.

## Key Features

- **9:16 Canvas with 4:5 Inner Card:** Optimized composition for maximum platform compatibility
- **Live Preview:** See your post update in real-time as you type
- **Smart Text Wrapping:** Automatically breaks long quotes into readable lines within the 4:5 card
- **Auto-Scaling Font:** Text automatically sizes to fit perfectly inside the 4:5 block
- **Color Customization:** Full control over background, card, text, and accent colors via color pickers
- **6 Community Templates:** Minimal, Vibrant, Dark, Ocean, Sunset, and Cyberpunk themes
- **Random Quote Generator:** Built-in quote library with one-click random selection
- **Dynamic Metadata:** Auto-syncs day, date, year, and progress percentage in pill-style tags
- **Customizable Header Tags:** Edit handle, day, date, year, and progress display
- **Customizable Footer:** Edit the signature text at the bottom of your post
- **One-Click Export:** Download as high-quality PNG image (1080x1920)

## Getting Started

1. **Open the app** in your browser
2. **Enter your quote** in the text area under the Quote tab
3. **Customize colors** using the color pickers or apply a template
4. **Edit metadata** under the Header tab (day tag, progress, date, year, handle)
5. **Preview your post** in real-time on the canvas
6. **Export** by clicking the "Export 9:16" button in the top right

### How the Layout Works

- The **9:16 canvas** provides the full vertical frame
- A **840x1080 pixel 4:5 card** sits centered with rounded corners
- Your quote text auto-sizes to fill the card's available space
- Header tags (day, progress, date, year, handle) float above the card
- Footer text sits below the card for attribution

## Technology Stack

- **HTML5 Canvas:** Core rendering engine
- **Tailwind CSS:** Utility-first styling framework
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** No frameworks needed, lightweight and fast
- **Color Picker API:** Native HTML color inputs for theme customization
